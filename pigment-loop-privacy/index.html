Pigment Loop: Art Sort Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Pigment Loop does not collect, store on any server, or share personal data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, or network access.

Stage progress and your language choice are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the app or using Reset progress removes them.

Questions: please use the developer contact shown on the Google Play listing.
